We are advertising a fully funded PhD studentship on atomic-precision acceptor qubits in silicon, available through the UCL EPSRC Doctoral Landscape Award (UELA) for 2027-28.

The application deadline is **12:00 (noon) UK time on Friday 8 January 2027**. This is a standard UELA project (ID 2531cs1110, EPSRC theme: Quantum Technologies), so it is open to applicants with Home fee status only.

Boron acceptors in silicon offer a distinct route to scalable quantum technologies. The hole they bind inherits strong spin-orbit coupling from the silicon valence band, which gives acceptor qubits advantages over donor qubits, including all-electrical control and readout. Our group recently achieved the first direct imaging of acceptor wave functions in silicon, and this project builds on that capability.

You will aim to achieve the first atomic-precision placement of individual acceptor dopants in silicon, using hydrogen desorption lithography and controlled boron trichloride surface processes. You will encapsulate the boron dopants by molecular beam epitaxy overgrowth, and use scanning tunnelling microscopy to probe the wave functions of the buried acceptors directly. In parallel, you may explore two-dimensional dopant layers beyond the equilibrium solubility limit, relevant for gates and contacts and for superconductivity in hole-doped silicon. The project includes collaboration with the Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI), Switzerland, on STM-based fabrication with extreme ultra-violet (EUV) tools, and you will present your work at international conferences.

We are looking for a motivated graduate in physics, chemistry or materials science, interested in surface science, instrumentation and quantum technology. Experience with UHV systems, scanning probe microscopy, photoelectron spectroscopy or density functional theory may be an advantage, but is not essential, and full training will be provided.

Applicants can apply to up to three UELA projects, and two references must be submitted by the deadline. UELA recommends having your application ready by 16 December 2026. Applications are made through the [UELA studentships page](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/study/doctoral-school/funding/epsrc-doctoral-funding/epsrc-prospective-doctoral-students/ucl-epsrc-doctoral-landscape-award-uela-studentships#projects), where the project catalogue is listed. Please also [contact us](/contact) early to discuss the project, and see our [Join page](/join/#uela) for other funding routes.
